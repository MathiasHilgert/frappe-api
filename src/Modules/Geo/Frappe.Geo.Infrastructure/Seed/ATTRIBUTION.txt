Geo reference data: sources, versions and licences
===================================================

The seed files in this folder are generated by the refresh scripts in eng/geo (run offline by a developer)
from the sources below. Retrieved 2026-10-31.

currencies.tsv (eng/geo/refresh_currencies.py)
- ISO 4217 currency codes, List One (current, published 2026-09-17) and List Three (historic, published
  2026-01-01), as published by SIX Group, the ISO 4217 maintenance agency:
  https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/data-standards.html
  Codes, numeric codes, minor units and English names. List Three carries no minor unit.
- Unicode CLDR 48 (cldr-json 48.0.0): currency display names in es and pt, and the supplemental
  currencyData fractions, which give the minor unit of List Three currencies (default 2 digits).
  Copyright (c) 1991-2025 Unicode, Inc. Licensed under the Unicode License v3:
  https://www.unicode.org/license.txt

countries.tsv (eng/geo/refresh_countries.py)
- Unicode CLDR 48 (cldr-json 48.0.0): codeMappings (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, alpha-3 and numeric codes),
  currencyData (current tender per territory) and territory display names in en, es and pt.
  Copyright (c) 1991-2025 Unicode, Inc. Licensed under the Unicode License v3:
  https://www.unicode.org/license.txt
- Google libphonenumber v9.0.42, resources/PhoneNumberMetadata.xml: international calling codes.
  Copyright (C) 2009 The Libphonenumber Authors. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
  https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
  https://github.com/google/libphonenumber

  Copyright (C) the iso-codes contributors. iso-codes is free software; you can redistribute it and/or modify it
  under the terms of the GNU Lesser General Public License as published by the Free Software Foundation; either
  version 2.1 of the License, or (at your option) any later version. It is distributed WITHOUT ANY WARRANTY;
  without even the implied warranty of MERCHANTABILITY or FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. See the GNU Lesser
  General Public License for more details: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
  The source of iso-codes, from which subdivisions.tsv is derived, is published at
  https://salsa.debian.org/iso-codes-team/iso-codes (tag v4.20.1); subdivisions.tsv is regenerated from it by
  eng/geo/refresh_subdivisions.py.

cities.tsv (eng/geo/refresh_cities.py)
- Fuente: Servicio de Normalización de Datos Geográficos de Argentina (Georef), INDEC. localidades.csv and
  provincias.csv from https://infra.datos.gob.ar/georef/ (published without a version; retrieved 2026-10-31).
  INDEC ids, official names, provinces and departments. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- The time zone of each locality comes from its province, mapped in eng/geo/ar-province-zones.tsv after the
  IANA tzdb zone.tab (public domain).

locales.tsv (eng/geo/refresh_locales.py)
- Unicode CLDR 48 (cldr-json 48.0.0): coverageLevels (the locales with modern coverage), language, script,
  territory and variant display names and the locale display pattern in each locale and in en, es and pt, and
  layout (text direction).
  Copyright (c) 1991-2025 Unicode, Inc. Licensed under the Unicode License v3:
  https://www.unicode.org/license.txt
